Section 3

API Quota Tracker

YouTube gives 10,000 units/day. search.list costs 100 units; most others cost 1 unit. QuotaTracker persists state to the DB, warns at the warn threshold, and aborts at the abort threshold.

Video Discovery: Search vs Playlist
